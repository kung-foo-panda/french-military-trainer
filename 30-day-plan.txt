# First — What I Meant By

## “You manually encode grammar progressively”

I did NOT mean you sit and type everything alone.

I meant:

Instead of importing the PDF blindly,
you **convert it into structured machine-readable grammar units**.

And yes — AI will help you do that.

### Example

PDF chapter:

> The Passé Composé with Avoir

We convert that into structured data like:

```ts
GrammarRule {
  id: "pc_avoir_core",
  category: "tense",
  title: "Passé Composé with Avoir",
  pattern: "subject + avoir + past participle",
  dependencies: ["present_avoir", "past_participle_regular"],
  examples: [],
  difficulty: 2
}
```

You do NOT write this manually.

You:

1. Paste grammar explanation
2. Ask AI:

   * Extract rule
   * Normalize structure
   * Identify dependencies
   * Generate verb lists
   * Generate irregular patterns
3. Review output
4. Store structured JSON

That’s what “encode progressively” means.

It becomes a knowledge graph of French grammar.

And that is extremely powerful.
