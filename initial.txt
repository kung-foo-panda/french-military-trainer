---

# 🔥 Intensive Learning Strategy (Important)

Instead of copying exercises blindly, the app should:

### 1️⃣ Convert exercises into patterns

Example:

PDF:

> Conjugate the verb “être” in passé composé.

App engine:

* Generates random subjects
* Generates random contexts
* Requires typed answer
* Evaluates answer strictly

So instead of 10 exercises:
You get unlimited dynamic exercises.

That’s how you make it intensive.

---

# 🧠 Example App Flow

### Module: Passé Composé

1. Explanation screen
2. Drill 1: Auxiliary selection (être vs avoir)
3. Drill 2: Past participle formation
4. Drill 3: Agreement rules
5. Drill 4: Mixed challenge mode
6. Weakness detection → repetition

---

# 💡 What Makes It "Intensive"?

Not number of exercises.
But:

* Timed drills
* Mistake clustering
* Weak area tracking
* Forced typing (no multiple choice)
* Delayed repetition (1d / 3d / 7d)
